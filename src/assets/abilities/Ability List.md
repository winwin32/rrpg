# Illusions 

## **False Image**

*Requires 1 Magical Secrets*

**Trigger**

When the caster attempts to create a magicial illusion 

**Range**

2 Hexes

**Whole**

Create an illusion

The illusion cannot:

1. Make sound

2. Move or change while active

3. Be larger than the caster in any dimension

4. Split into multiple illusions

⠀

If an enemy has reason to doubt the illusion, roll **Mind** vs target **Toughness**

On a success, the illusion remains

On a failure, the illusion is dispelled for all targets

**Broken**

Dispel all current illusions

## **Dominate**

*Requires 5 Magical Secrets, Memory Fog, Deja Vu and Enflame*

**Trigger**

When the caster attempts to control the mind of another living being

**Cost**

* 1 Action Token

* 1 Turn

**Range**

Adjacent

**Whole**

Choose a target

Roll **Mind** vs target **Toughness**

On a success, the caster controls the target until the end of the round

The caster may use the target’s movement, attacks, and abilities by spending Action Tokens normally

The caster cannot move, attack, or use abilities during this time

Gain **Tough x2**

Dispel all current illusions

The target remembers being controlled

**Broken**

If the caster is controlling a target, control immediately ends

---
